# Red-team review, 2026-09-27

Five independent reviews, with no API calls: statistics, controls and baselines, harness, hostile reader, and
reproduction. The reviewers' scratch scripts are in the session scratchpad (tau_stats, tau_baselines, tau_harness,
tau_repro).

## What survives

1. **Instruction scope changes wrongful refusals.** This is the solid result. Setting: τ² airline, single-decision
   replays (one JSON answer, no tool calls).
   - **Gemini 3.8 Flash:** "Before deciding on a cancellation or refund, recompute…" gave 55/96 wrongful refusals of
     overpaid customers. "Before telling the customer yes or no…" gave 8/96 (−49 pp [−59, −38]; paired McNemar,
     p = 1.8e−13; two halves agree).
   - **Six paraphrases (preregistered secondary):** 58–84% for action-scoped wordings vs 0–8% for answer-scoped.
     Generalising to wording *types* rests on 3 vs 3 sentences.
   - **Sonnet 5:** 37/48 → 21/48 (p = 8.6e−4).
   - **Opus 5.5:** 0/24 under every wording (run 151002). This null was omitted from FINDINGS, the paper and the
     MEMO, and must be added.
   - **Harness verified for this run:** prompt hashes match the frozen manifest; the design is interleaved;
     perturbations are exactly ±fee; the JSON action matches the transcript in 3,672/3,672 cases.
2. **Stock policy: nobody reconciles unprompted.** 0/72 airline, 1/144 retail. Report this as a "trust rate": the
   stock policy doesn't ask for audits, so this is not an error.
3. **One contradiction clause switches Opus from 0% to 100% transfers** (12 bases, p = 5e−4; replicated with a
   neutral script and in retail, 48/48 vs 1/48). Scoped to one clause wording.
4. **Agents follow the policy's fee:** 0/36 correct records held. Descriptive.

## What does not survive

- **The mechanism "checks before yes, skips no" / "lopsided vigilance".**
  - Better explained by literal scope: "a refusal isn't a cancellation or refund".
  - Every trigger containing "no"/"refuse" works; "your decision" still leaks 40%; "any answer" leaks 31%.
  - The reviewers disagree on how many refusals mention the discrepancy (39/288 in reasoning summaries vs 0/298
     in messages). Re-check before citing either.
- **Generality / "task-specific".** Every retail task was at ceiling: 0 errors in 1,752 decisions. That ceiling was
  known about 1 minute before the preregistration was frozen. Retail checks state the rule verbatim; the airline
  check doesn't. So generality is untested.
- **Effort rule.** The retail analogue forces the "no" path through the corrupted number, so it can't show the
  airline mechanism. The rule is untestable, not failed. Stop quoting "CI 0.0–0.0".
- **Conversations: "the gap disappeared (0/8 vs 0/8)".**
  - The raw harness output is 2/8 vs 1/8. The 0/8 came from a hand reclassification.
  - The Flash simulator hangs up at the transfer offer, and crashed episodes are labelled as refusals.
  - Flash used τ²'s calculator in 12/16 episodes, a tool the single-decision format doesn't allow.
  - So the single-decision effect may be a format effect. Report it as a stress test.
- **Checking costs legitimate customers (83–100% benign holds), and the provenance story.** The benign credit line
  plants a second anomaly (a payment_id not in the user's methods; Opus cites it in 9–11 of 11–12 holds). The
  counting rule was tuned and tested on the same 12 bases, and the "forged" control can't fail.
- **F5 ledger, 24/24.** The ledger output prints the verdict ("insurance fee paid but insurance marked no"). This
  tests reading, not checking.
- **Pooled OR ≈ 5,000.** The model is quasi-separated, and the prereg says to report "not established". Delete it.
- **Retail "catch rates the same whoever loses".** Under the checklist, Sonnet was 17/24 vs 24/24 (p = 0.016).
- **n = 12 headline contrasts.** Opus benign holds moved from 11/12 to 2/12 just by listing an unused tool, so noise
  exceeds several effects.
